About me

I am currently a PhD student at the University of Michigan. I graduated from Minerva University with a physics and computer science degree in 2022.

During Minerva, I had academic and professional experiences in South Korea, India, England, and the United States.

After college, I worked for two years at Argonne National Laboratory with the Cosmological Physics & Advanced Computing group.

At Michigan, I work with Type Ia supernovae, fundamental tests, and DESI.
